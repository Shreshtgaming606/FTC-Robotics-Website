About the team

Built to
learn.

The Viltrumites are an FTC robotics team where engineering, software, creativity, and teamwork meet one shared challenge: build something better together.

01 Who we are

A team shaped by the process.

We work through the full cycle of competitive robotics: understanding the challenge, exploring ideas, building mechanisms, programming robot behavior, testing under pressure, and improving what we learn.

FTC gives us a space to practice real engineering habits—clear communication, careful iteration, problem solving, and responsible teamwork. Every build session is a chance to learn new technology and help one another grow.

Our mission

Combine every discipline into one competitive robot.

We bring engineering, programming, creativity, and teamwork together to design competitive robots while continuously learning and improving.

What we do

Many skills. One system.

Each area supports the others. A promising idea becomes valuable when it can be built, programmed, tested, and used with purpose.

Our values

How we show up.

The way we work together matters as much as the machine we bring to the field.

Teamwork

Share knowledge, listen closely, and build trust across every part of the team.

Innovation

Look beyond the first answer and explore ideas worth testing.

Persistence

Treat setbacks as information and keep working toward a better solution.

Creativity

Combine practical thinking with imagination to approach hard problems.

Gracious Professionalism

Compete with respect, help others, and value the people behind the work.

Continuous Improvement

Review, learn, refine, and return to the next iteration with purpose.
