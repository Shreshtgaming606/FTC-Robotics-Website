About the team

Driven to
improve.

The Viltrumites are FTC Team #36082 — a group of builders, programmers, problem-solvers, and teammates working together to turn ideas into a competitive robot.

01 Who we are

Different strengths. One team.

What defines our team is the variety of strengths each member brings. From programming and troubleshooting to mechanical design, building, and strategy, we combine those skills to solve problems and improve the robot as a team.

For us, FTC is a place to turn ideas into something real. Every design, line of code, test run, and rebuild gives us another opportunity to learn, communicate better, and make the robot stronger than it was before.

Our mission

Turn teamwork into performance.

Our goal is to bring together coding, engineering, creativity, and teamwork to build a robot we are proud of — then keep testing, refining, and improving it throughout the season.

What we do

From idea to competition.

Our process connects every part of the team. Ideas are designed, built, programmed, tested, and refined until each system works together with a clear purpose.

01

Design

Break down the game challenge, explore possible solutions, and choose ideas that are realistic, effective, and worth building.

02

Engineering

Transform concepts into reliable mechanical and electrical systems that work together as one robot.

03

Programming

Develop dependable robot behavior for autonomous routines, driver control, and the systems that connect software to hardware.

04

Testing

Put every mechanism and program through repeated testing, identify weak points, and use the results to guide the next improvement.

05

Strategy

Think beyond individual mechanisms and plan how the robot, drivers, and team decisions can work together during a match.

06

Competition

Bring our preparation to the field, adapt when things change, support one another, and compete with Gracious Professionalism.

Our values

How we show up.

A strong robot matters, but so does the way we work together while building it.

Teamwork

Share ideas, communicate clearly, and make sure every member can contribute to the team's progress.

Innovation

Stay open to new approaches and test ideas instead of settling for the first solution that works.

Persistence

Use failed tests and setbacks as information, then keep working until we find a better solution.

Creativity

Approach difficult problems with both practical engineering and creative thinking.

Gracious Professionalism

Compete with respect, support others, and represent our team with the spirit of Gracious Professionalism.

Continuous Improvement

Keep reviewing what works, fixing what does not, and improving each version of the robot throughout the season.
